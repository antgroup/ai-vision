# AI Vision Code of Conduct

This code covers project-managed issues, pull requests, discussions, meetings, and other collaboration spaces, including contributions prepared with coding agents. It follows the [bootstrap governance agreement's acceptance conditions](GOVERNANCE.md). Named report handlers and private contact routes are [pending confirmation](GOVERNANCE.md#pending-owner-and-contact-confirmations); this document does not appoint anyone or activate a reporting service.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully across backgrounds, experience levels, employers, and technical views.
- Discuss ideas and behavior using specific evidence. Accept constructive feedback and allow others to disagree.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and the time of contributors and maintainers. Reporting an issue does not create an obligation to implement a fix.
- Take responsibility for material submitted through automated tools and correct harmful or misleading content.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, and retaliation for good-faith reports are unacceptable. Persistent disruption of project collaboration is also unacceptable. Technical disagreement and evidence-based criticism are welcome.

## Reporting concerns

Keep incident details, private messages, identities, and evidence out of public issues and PRs. Use a private route once its recipient and purpose have been confirmed. Until then, an existing maintainer may be asked to arrange one; a public request should say only that a private conduct-reporting contact is needed. If making even that request would expose someone, retain the report privately until a suitable route is confirmed.

Once a route is available, share the relevant context, dates, links or evidence, and any confidentiality or conflict-of-interest concerns. Provide only information needed to understand the incident. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Handling and reconsideration

Core maintainers arrange an uninvolved handler with appropriate authority. A person directly involved in the report must not make the final decision. Limit access to those handling the matter and share only necessary information; do not promise absolute confidentiality.

Authorized maintainers may request corrections, remove harmful content, or restrict participation proportionately to the conduct and risk. Record the rationale privately where needed to protect participants. Explain the outcome and reconsideration route to the affected people to the extent appropriate without exposing others' private information.

A participant may request reconsideration by an uninvolved maintainer through a confirmed private route. The primary handler, alternate handler, and contact route still need confirmation. No response-time or staffing commitment is established by this document.
